Questions

Where does the data come from?

From the public disclosures members of Congress must file under the STOCK Act with the Clerk of the House and the Secretary of the Senate, as published in open datasets built from those filings, plus the official congressional directory for names, parties and photos. Every trade traces back to an official filing.

Is this legal and is it affiliated with Congress?

Yes, it is legal: these records are published precisely so the public can read them. Hillradar is independent and not affiliated with any government body, party or lawmaker.

How fast do new filings appear?

Usually within hours of publication. Note that the law gives lawmakers up to 45 days to file after a trade; we show that delay on every record and mark late filings.

Is this investment advice?

No. Hillradar is a research and transparency tool about public records. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Read our Disclaimer.
