2. Where the data comes from

  • Trades are self-reported by members of Congress in Periodic Transaction Reports required by the STOCK Act, published by the US House Clerk and the US Senate Office of Public Records.
  • We ingest those filings via public-domain datasets that parse the official documents, and refresh them on a schedule. A link to the original filing is kept with each trade where available.
  • Lawmaker names, parties, states and photos come from the public unitedstates/congress-legislators and unitedstates/images projects.
  • Hillradar AI is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the US Congress, any government body, any lawmaker, or any company mentioned.

3. Known limitations of disclosure data

  • Delay: lawmakers may file up to 45 days after a trade, and some file late. By the time a trade is public, the price may have moved.
  • Ranges, not amounts: filings disclose dollar ranges (e.g. $15,001 – $50,000). Our "volume" figures use the upper bound of each range and are estimates.
  • Ownership: many trades are by a spouse, dependent child or a joint account, or by a manager without the lawmaker's direction.
  • Parsing errors: official filings are PDFs and web forms; automated parsing can mis-read a ticker, date or amount. Amended filings may not be reflected immediately.
  • Coverage: some assets (bonds, funds, private holdings) have no ticker and are excluded from stock-level analysis.

4. What AI Signals and track records are

  • A signal is a statistical summary: how many lawmakers bought or sold a ticker in a window, how much, from which parties, how recently, and how the buyers' past disclosed trades performed. These factors are combined into a 0–100 score. The weights are our own design and may change.
  • A track record (hit rate, average excess return) is computed from the historical performance of a lawmaker's disclosed buys over 6 months versus the market, using a public research dataset. It is backward-looking, based on small samples for many members, and ignores transaction costs, taxes and timing.
  • AI theses are generated by a language model from the facts above (or a template when AI is off). They can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date and are not reviewed by a human analyst.
  • Historically, only about half of congressional buys beat the market over 6 months. A signal increases attention, not certainty.
Treat every signal as a starting point for your own research, never as an instruction.
